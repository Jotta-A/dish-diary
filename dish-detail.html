The Critic's Consensus

A masterclass in restraint and indulgence. The pasta itself is ethereal—rolled so thin it's nearly translucent, yet retaining a perfect, springy bite. But it's the emulsion of aged Parmigiano-Reggiano and butter, crowned with an earthy blizzard of black truffle, that elevates this from a mere plate of noodles to a profound culinary experience.
